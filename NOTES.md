## What the data actually said

Initially, I expected total mileage and vehicle age to be strong predictors of breakdowns. However, the analysis showed that they made almost no meaningful difference between vehicles that broke down and those that did not. The strongest predictor was the number of kilometres travelled since the last service. Vehicles that had gone a long distance without servicing had a much higher breakdown rate. Higher average daily kilometres and a higher load factor were also associated with more breakdowns. The main takeaway was that how recently and heavily a vehicle was used mattered more than its total mileage or age.
